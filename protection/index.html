What's included

  • EDR on your device. Sophos endpoint detection and response, installed straight from Sophos.
  • MDR included. Sophos Managed Detection & Response runs alongside EDR on your device - it needs to stay active for the warranty below to apply.
  • Warranty coverage. Up to $1M under Sophos's terms.
  • 12 months, no cost to you. Selected members only.

How it works

  1. Apply below.
  2. We review and reply.
  3. You get an official Sophos link. We never send files or ask you to download anything in chat.
  4. Install, and book a setup call if you want one.

Protection reduces risk, it does not make you unhackable. We don't collect wallet addresses, balances or documents. *Warranty terms are set by Sophos, not Vanneck.
