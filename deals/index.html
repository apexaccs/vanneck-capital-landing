Skalor

$1.5M · Aug 2026

Clearing infrastructure for the agentic economy. Skalor turns AI agents into governed economic actors through programmable mandates - its TypeScript SDK runs seven fiduciary checks in a single call and supports x402 natively.

Source: Medium (Provenance), Oct 22, 2026

Okto

$750K · Nov 2025

Self-custody DeFi wallet with multi-chain support. Users swap tokens across chains, provide liquidity and buy crypto by card or bank transfer from one app. Okto secures keys with multi-party computation and auto-converts tokens so users don't need separate gas tokens.

Source: X - @Firstprint_HQ

Cambria

$125K

Real-money sandbox MMO - players put crypto at risk in a PvP and PvE economy with onchain significance. Three game modes (Box, Draw, Classic), onchain metrics on public dashboards. Moved into soft launch in April.

Source: Telegram - ZeroBlock Feed

Mises

$1M · Series A · Mar 2023

Mises Browser is the world's first mobile Web3 browser supporting Chrome extensions on both Android and iOS, letting users run their existing extension wallets and dApps directly from a phone.

Source: Dealroom - Vanneck Capital portfolio

Matrix

$700K · Seed · Oct 2025

Matrix is a financial technology company (matrix.app) building payment infrastructure aimed at replacing wallet addresses with readable identities and making cross-border money movement as fast as sending a message.
