What we do

One check. Everything a project needs comes with it.

The investment is the foundation of the relationship. Every additional service below is included at no extra cost and is never sold on its own.

Capital

Direct checks from $100K to $2M. Form to term sheet in 14 days when the fit is clear, 3 to 6 weeks for a standard review. SAFE, SAFT, Token Warrant or hybrid instruments.

Listings & exchanges

Warm introductions to Tier 1 and Tier 2 centralized exchanges, plus concentrated-liquidity DEX setup, at negotiated rates through our partner network.

Market making

Introductions to market-making partners at preferred rates, across concentrated liquidity, stable pools, protocol-owned liquidity and active LP management.

Distribution

Go-to-market strategy, launch sequencing, KOL campaigns, PR and community growth, plus introductions to ecosystem partners and integrations.

Advisory & network

Access to an advisory board across DeFi, regulation, exchange strategy and tokenomics, and warm introductions to 40+ investors, family offices and portfolio founders.

Security

Smart contract audits covered from $30K to $100K+, multi-sig setup, and a mandatory security review before closing that protects both sides and stays with the team afterward.
