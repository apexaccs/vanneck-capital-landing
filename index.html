FAQ

Questions founders actually ask

What is Vanneck Capital?

A direct, early-stage Web3 investor using a cornerstone model: one check includes capital plus the network — exchanges, market makers, auditors — instead of charging for each separately.

How is this different from an accelerator?

We don't run cohorts or charge for programming. We write a direct check and bring the relationships that would otherwise cost extra, included with it.

What stage and check size do you invest at?

Pre-seed through seed. $550K average check (MENA $750K, Asia $500K), up to $2M via SPV for stronger rounds.

How fast is the process?

As little as 14 days from application to term sheet in hot cases, 10 days from a yes to the LOI. Diligence runs through a data room and an investment committee, not weeks of back and forth.

What do you look for in a team?

A founding team that can execute, real traction or a technical edge, and a credible path to TGE. We'd rather see an honest data room than a polished one.

What's included with the check beyond capital?

Audit coverage, exchange listings, market making, distribution, advisory and security — the full breakdown is in What's included.

Do you charge extra for listings or market making?

No added fees. We share our own retainer rates with portfolio companies — $15K–$25K saved per $1M, included with the check.

Is the smart-contract audit really free?

The audit is covered by us, $30K–$100K+ depending on scope, plus multi-sig setup and a $1M breach warranty.

Do you help with the next round?

Yes — data room prep and warm intros to 40+ investors. 19 of 19 Accelerator Fund deals raised follow-on within 12 months.

Where is Vanneck based, and who's behind it?

Vanneck Limited is incorporated in the UK. The team's background spans Binance, Bybit, Ripple and Solana Foundation — see Team and the legal details in the footer.

How do I apply?

Use the form here with a deck or one-pager and a link to what you're building. We review async and come back before scheduling a call — no application fee.
