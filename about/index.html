About Vanneck Capital

Vanneck Capital is a direct, early-stage Web3 investor and accelerator, operated by Vanneck & Partners Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14759731), investing capital alongside audits, exchange listings, market making and introductions as a single check.

Vanneck Capital is not VanEck Capital, the private-markets investment platform operated by VanEck Associates Corporation (a separate US asset manager), and is not affiliated with VanEck, VanEck Capital, VanEck Associates Corporation, with any company registered as "Vanneck Ltd," with Vanneck Residential LLP, with the website vanneckpartners.com, or with any other company using a similar name. Vanneck Capital's only website is vanneck.xyz, and "Vanneck" on this site refers only to Vanneck Capital / Vanneck & Partners Ltd as described on this page.

What we do

We write direct checks into pre-seed and seed Web3 teams, averaging $550K, with the investment itself as the cornerstone of the relationship. Everything else - smart-contract audits, exchange listing introductions, market making, go-to-market support, security and warm intros to our investor network - comes with the check at no extra cost. We do not sell services separately, and we receive no fees, tokens or equity from these service partners - our only economics come from the investment itself. Earlier-stage teams can enter through our Accelerator Fund. See What's included and our blog for the detail.

Where we're registered

Vanneck & Partners Ltd is incorporated in the United Kingdom (company number 14759731, incorporated 27 March 2023) and headquartered in Canary Wharf, London. Full registered-office details and the Companies House record are in the site footer.
