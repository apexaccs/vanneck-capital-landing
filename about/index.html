About Vanneck Capital

Vanneck Capital is a direct, early-stage Web3 investor and accelerator, operated by Vanneck & Partners Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales (company number 14759731), investing capital alongside audits, exchange listings, market making and introductions as a single check.

Vanneck Capital is not affiliated with VanEck (the US-based asset manager, VanEck Associates Corporation), with any company registered as "Vanneck Ltd," with the website vanneckpartners.com, or with any other company using a similar name. Vanneck Capital's only website is vanneck.xyz, and "Vanneck" on this site refers only to Vanneck Capital / Vanneck & Partners Ltd as described on this page.

What we do

We write direct checks into pre-seed and seed Web3 teams, averaging $550K, with the investment itself as the cornerstone of the relationship. Everything else - smart-contract audits, exchange listing introductions, market making, go-to-market support, security and warm intros to our investor network - comes with the check at no extra cost. We do not sell services separately. Earlier-stage teams can enter through our Accelerator Fund. See What's included and our blog for the detail.

Where we're registered

Vanneck & Partners Ltd is incorporated in the United Kingdom (company number 14759731, incorporated 27 March 2023). Full registered-office details and the Companies House record are in the site footer.
