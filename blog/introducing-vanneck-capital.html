Introducing Vanneck Capital

Vanneck Capital is a Web3 investor and accelerator. We write the check first and build around it.

How we invest

Our direct checks average $550K, with a working range of $100K to $500K. The average is $750K in MENA and $500K in Asia. For larger rounds we use SPVs of up to $2M, and we can take bigger tickets to partner funds. Earlier-stage teams can enter through the Accelerator Fund at $100K to $235K. We use SAFE, SAFT, token warrant and hybrid instruments, and token allocation follows the same logic as equity.

The check is the deal

Investment is the cornerstone of every Vanneck deal. Audit, listings, market making, go-to-market support, security and introductions to investors and exchanges come with it at no extra cost. We do not sell services separately. Our motto is giving, not selling.

Track record

So far we have made 45 equity and 62 token investments, and our portfolio has raised $285M.

Who should apply

Founders who are building and raising, and who want an investor that does more than wire money. Apply at vanneck.xyz.
