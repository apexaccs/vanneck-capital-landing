Build your data room before you need it

A data room is a shared folder with everything an investor needs to check your company. Founders usually build one in a hurry, after an investor asks. That is the slowest way to do it. A data room built early costs a few hours and removes weeks from a raise.

What goes in

  1. Cap table, fully diluted. Every shareholder, option, SAFE, SAFT and token commitment, with vesting status. Include anything unvested that is held by someone who has left. Investors find it anyway, and finding it first is better than being found out.
  2. Corporate documents. Incorporation papers, shareholder agreements, board minutes and any prior financing documents.
  3. Financials and KPIs. Monthly figures for the last twelve months and the numbers for the last thirty days. A raw spreadsheet is faster to read than a designed deck.
  4. Product and security. Audit reports and their status, what is running in production without an audit, and a short note on how keys and admin access are handled.
  5. Token plan. Allocation, vesting, what is released in the first twelve months after TGE, and who holds it.
  6. Legal. IP assignments from every contributor, key contracts and any open disputes.
  7. Team. Roles, contracts and who is full-time.

How to organize it

  • One folder per section, numbered in the order above.
  • File names that say what the file is and its date, for example 2026-10_cap-table.xlsx.
  • One summary page at the top with links and the date it was last updated.
  • Read-only access for investors, with a record of who was invited.
  • Nothing in it that you would not want a counterparty to read. If a document needs explaining, add a short note beside it.

Keep it alive

A data room goes stale quickly. Update the cap table and the monthly numbers when they change, not when someone asks. Put a recurring reminder in your calendar for the first of each month.

Why it matters

An organized data room shortens diligence, and it shows an investor how the company is run. Questions that would take a week to answer take an hour. It also forces you to find your own problems before anyone else does.

At Vanneck, data room preparation for a follow-on raise is part of the support that comes with the investment. Founders who already have one in place start that conversation ahead.
